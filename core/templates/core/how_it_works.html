How it works

Four steps from incident to insight — designed so a miner can report in under 60 seconds.

01

Capture

Worker opens the app, picks a hazard type, snaps a photo, adds a description. GPS and time are captured automatically.

02

Review

The supervisor gets an instant alert and inspects the photo, description and location.

03

Resolve

A guided checklist walks the supervisor through securing the area. They upload an evidence photo and mark resolved.

04

Analyse

The map and dashboard show where hazards cluster, so recurring problems get spotted before the next incident.
