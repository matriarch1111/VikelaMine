Get Started How It Works

Making Safety Reporting Easier

VikelaMine provides a central platform for recording, managing and reviewing mining safety hazards.

🚨

Record Hazards

Workers can report hazards and unsafe conditions using structured safety reports.

🔧

Resolve Issues

Supervisors can review reported hazards and track actions taken to address them.

📊

Prevent Future Risks

Recorded safety information can be reviewed to identify recurring hazards and areas requiring attention.

Key Features

The platform brings important safety information together in one system.

📋

Hazard Reporting

Create reports for unsafe conditions and mining hazards.

📍

Location Information

Associate reports with locations to help identify where hazards are occurring.

📸

Evidence

Support safety reports with information and evidence about the reported hazard.

🗺️

Safety Map

View reported hazards geographically and identify areas where safety issues have been recorded.

📊

Safety Summaries

Review reported hazards and their status through safety summaries.

🆘

Emergency Alert

Provide an emergency alert mechanism for situations requiring immediate attention.

About VikelaMine

VikelaMine is focused on improving the way mining safety information is recorded and managed.

Instead of relying only on informal reporting or paper-based records, VikelaMine provides a central digital platform where safety hazards can be recorded, reviewed and followed through to resolution.
