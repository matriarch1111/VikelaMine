FAQ

Does VikelaMine work without internet?

The report flow captures GPS and photos locally; reports can be finished when back online.

What does the panic button do?

It broadcasts an emergency alert with your live GPS location to supervisors instantly.

Can a worker resolve a hazard?

No. Only supervisors can resolve hazards — workers report and stay informed.

Is the data ever deleted?

Resolved hazards stay in the database permanently to build a long-term safety history.
